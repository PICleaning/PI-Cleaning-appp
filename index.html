App Guide
PI Cleaning Services · Employee Guide
🚨
Do NOT Force-Close This App
If you force-close or swipe away this app during your shift, the system will not receive your clock-out and your shift timer will stop updating. This may affect your pay records. Always use the Clock Out button to end your shift properly.
Mon
How to Know Your Location Is Being Tracked
When you are clocked in, look at the Tiffany blue pill on your shift timer showing the current day — for example "Monday". As long as that pill is visible and glowing teal, your location is actively being tracked. If it disappears and reverts to plain text, tracking has paused (e.g. you are on break or GPS is temporarily unavailable).
📍
Always Capture GPS When Adding a Job
When you tap + Add Job, you must tap the GPS button before confirming. This records your exact location at that job site and is required for each new location.
☕
Taking a Break
Tap Break in the top-right when you start your break. Tap Resume when you're back. Break time is automatically deducted from your total hours. Location tracking pauses during breaks.
💾
If You Close & Reopen the App
If you close the browser tab and reopen it during your shift, your session will be automatically restored — your timer continues from where it left off. Your name and Employee ID are remembered for 7 days so you only need to enter your job address each shift.
⏹
When Location Tracking Stops
Your location is only tracked during active shifts. Tracking stops the moment you tap Clock Out, or automatically after a 12-hour maximum shift. No data is collected outside of your shift hours.
Privacy Policy
PI Cleaning Services · Location Data
📍 Location Tracking During Shifts
Your GPS location is collected automatically when you clock in and continues to be recorded throughout your active shift. This data is used solely to verify attendance, confirm job site presence, and support workforce management operations.
⏹ When Tracking Stops
Location tracking stops immediately when you clock out of your shift, or when your shift is automatically ended after the maximum shift duration. No location data is collected outside of active shift hours.
🗓 Data Retention
All location coordinates recorded during your shift are automatically and permanently deleted from our systems within 14 days of collection. No location data is retained beyond this period.
🔐 How Your Data Is Used
Location data is transmitted securely to PI Cleaning Services' internal records system. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for any purpose outside of employment and operational needs.
✅ Your Consent
By clocking in to this application, you acknowledge and consent to the collection of your GPS location data under the terms described in this policy, in accordance with applicable Australian privacy legislation.
For any questions about your data or to request access to your records, please contact your manager or the PI Cleaning Services administration team.
